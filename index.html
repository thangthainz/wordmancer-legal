Wordmancer

"In the beginning was the Word. Then came the Silence."

Wordmancer is a premium dark-academia word roguelite for iOS. Build spells out of grimoire pages, charm tiles, and rare modifiers across six antes of escalating boss fights. A daily verse leaderboard, an endless echo arc, and 35+ achievements weave through the run loop.
